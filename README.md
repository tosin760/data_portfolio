🛒 E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce company's transactional data to uncover insights into sales performance, product profitability, customer behavior, customer retention, and churn.

The analysis was performed using MySQL and was designed around realistic business questions that a data analyst might receive from sales, marketing, and management teams.

Rather than focusing only on writing SQL queries, this project demonstrates my ability to:

-Translate business questions into analytical queries
-Work with relational datasets
-Clean and aggregate transactional data
-Develop meaningful business KPIs
-Analyze trends over time
-Segment customers
-Measure retention and churn
-Interpret analytical results from a business perspective

🎯 Business Objectives

The project addresses the following business questions:

Sales Performance
-How much revenue has the company generated?
-How much revenue comes from completed orders?
-How is revenue changing over time?
-What is the month-over-month and year-over-year growth?

Product Performance
-Which products generate the most revenue?
-Which products generate the most profit?
-Which products have the strongest profit margins?
-Which products generate significant revenue while maintaining healthy margins?

Customer Analytics
-Who are the company's highest-value customers?
-What is the Average Order Value (AOV)?
-What percentage of customers are repeat customers?
-Which customer segments generate the most revenue?

Retention & Churn
-How well does the company retain customers?
-How many customers are at risk?
-What percentage of customers have churned?
-Are high-revenue customers more likely to churn?
